    .includes() — is it there?
    
      
    A question with a yes-or-no answer, which is why it fits inside an if. It compares the whole item, so capital letters and extra characters count.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        On an array: does it hold this item?
        
    
        On a string: does it contain these characters?
